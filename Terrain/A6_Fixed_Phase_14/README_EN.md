# Grass ↔ flagstone: 14 fixed-phase modules

Use a 64×64 grid, native scale and nearest sampling. G = grass_base_64; S = flagstone_base_64. Twelve transitions provide four straight edges, four concave and four convex corners. Resolve keys through LAYOUTS.json/modules/path and copy its grids; the numbered placement guide shows module positions.

Use the included 4×4 courtyard, four concave examples and 11×8 courtyard/road. Use their layouts as placement examples. For the courtyard upper row use outer_tl / top / top / outer_tr; fill the interior with S and the exterior with G.

Keep the two bundled base crops and their grid phase. Do not substitute random crops, rotate freely or mix arbitrary textures. Repeated grass clumps and brown soil tones remain. This is a small fixed-layout kit, not complete random autotiling or a plugin.
